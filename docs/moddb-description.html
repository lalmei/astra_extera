AstraExtera gives each Vintage Story save a generated astronomical setting. The server authors and stores a galaxy, a location inside it, a visible star field and a local planetary system. Every client receives the same result. AstraTerra draws that sky and supplies the observing tools.

Press Ctrl+Shift+S to inspect the generated galaxy and system. Then use AstraTerra's Sky Disc, telescope, Sextant and Astrolabe to observe the sky, record measurements and draw your own constellations.

🧩 Requirements: Vintage Story 1.22.2 or newer and AstraTerra 0.10.0 or newer. Enable both mods.

⚠ What stays Vintage Story's

The generated radius, gravity, temperature and orbital facts describe the setting shown in the galaxy panel. They do not change terrain, ores, climate, player gravity, the calendar, day length, seasons or world generation. The visible ordinary moon is hidden, but moonlight and the calendar phase still come from Vintage Story.

Start observing

  1. Join a world and wait for the server's sky to arrive.
  2. Press Ctrl+Shift+S. The Galaxy panel binding can be changed in Controls.
  3. Open H > Astronomy for recipes and item instructions.
  4. Make a Sky Disc to mark sunrise or sunset, or use a telescope with a writable book in the left hand and ink and quill in the inventory to draw a constellation.
  5. Use a Sextant to record a light's altitude and bearing. Compare sightings to find what moved against the fixed stars.

A light may be below the horizon now, visible only at another hour or season, or unable to rise at the current latitude. Comets have limited return windows. Time, world position, open sky, darkness and weather all matter to an observation.

The generated sky

▾  🌌 Galaxy, location and stars

The generator usually creates an analytic spiral galaxy and sometimes an elliptical. It chooses a metal-rich habitable site, a host star and a local system. These are astronomical metadata, not a replacement for Vintage Story's terrain simulation.

The visible catalog is sampled from the site's stellar density, a luminosity function and dust extinction. It keeps stars down to magnitude 6.5 until the 10,000-star budget is full. Unresolved light becomes a generated galactic band behind the stars.

▾  🪐 Planets, comets and showers

The local system can contain inner rocky worlds, a shepherd gas giant beyond the snow line, another gas giant and outer ice giants. Their generated Keplerian elements produce the points of light that move against the stars.

Each save also receives two to four returning comet apparitions and meteor showers associated with them. The planet elements, comet tracks and shower schedules are game-scale approximations, not a gravitational integration.

▾  🌙 Planet worlds and moon worlds

A planet world can have zero to three generated moons. Each rises and sets according to its generated month.

On a moon world, the playable world is tidally locked to a gas giant. The giant stays fixed in the sky, changes phase with the sun and can span tens of degrees. Sibling moons may cross its face or pass behind it. Twelve giant images, fifty-four moon images and sixteen ring images are chosen and composited from the generated body's color and geometry.

▾  📖 Observations and constellations

The generated catalog has no inherited Earth constellation figures or sky culture. Players draw their own lines into books or cut one figure into a Sky Disc. The server stores the sampled stars so normal reloads preserve the numeric star IDs used by those records.

AstraExtera does not yet generate deep-sky objects. Generated companion planets remain points in the telescope because their panel portraits are diagrams, not telescope plates.

Configuration and known limits

  • AstraExtera has no configuration file. AstraTerra's ModConfig/astraterra.json controls its renderer, instruments, coordinate grids, clock and longitude-aware sun.
  • AstraTerra's shipped Earth Milky Way is a separate render pass. If two bands overlap, set MilkyWayBrightness to 0.0 with the client closed, then restart. This setting disables AstraTerra's band, not AstraExtera's generated glow.
  • .stars starfield vanilla does not disable AstraExtera's glow or nearby bodies.
  • MoonArt cannot restore the ordinary moon while AstraExtera's near-body catalog is active.
  • On a non-realistic-climate world, Vintage Story may report one latitude everywhere. The sky then does not tilt as you travel north or south.

Server commands

/astraextera galaxy reports the current cosmology and local system. An administrator with controlserver can run /astraextera reroll for a new random cosmology or /astraextera reroll 42 for a signed 64-bit seed. Omit the slash in the server console.

A reroll stores and broadcasts a replacement galaxy placement, star field and local sky. Terrain and the world-generation seed do not change. Existing constellation records and star names remain, but their star IDs now refer to the replacement catalog, so old drawings change.

AstraTerra and development

AstraTerra is the sky engine and observing game. AstraExtera is the save-specific catalog author. The current integration uses AstraTerra's full-catalog replacement API; it is not an additive catalog registry.

Source, issue tracking, player instructions and the architecture guide are in the AstraExtera repository. Automated tests cover generation, serialization, export, geometry and asset contracts. Visual behavior and mixed-mod compatibility still require in-game testing.
